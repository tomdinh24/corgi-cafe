## How it works

# Say what would be useful now.

Share just enough context for a thoughtful introduction. This is not a resume builder or an inbox for strangers.

1. **Give Corgi context**  
   Confirm your background, then say what you would enjoy discussing during this visit.

2. **Set your boundaries**  
   Choose what you can offer, what is off limits, and how available you are today.

3. **Meet when there is a fit**  
   If both people are eligible, Corgi introduces you at the same time with a reason to talk.

---

## The product contract

# Relevance is not permission.

Context can help Corgi understand fit. An introduction is eligible only when both people are present, available, visible, and open to the same kind of conversation.

**Relevance helps rank:** Shared problems, complementary experience, and ask-to-offer fit.

**Permission decides eligibility:** Availability, interaction boundaries, visibility, and session expiration.

---

## A thoughtful host

# A reason, not a random name.

Every introduction explains who the person is, why the conversation could be useful to both of you, what to start with, and why the interaction is appropriate now.

**Example introduction**

**Strong mutual fit — Until 3:30 PM**

Corgi would like you to meet **Maya, an early GTM operator**.

You are both working through founder-led GTM. Maya has helped two developer-tool teams make their first sales hire and is open to exchanging tactical lessons now.

**Start here:** What changed when founder-led sales stopped scaling?

---

## Quality over volume

# No weak matches.

Sometimes the right answer is to wait. Corgi will not surface someone who is unavailable, outside your boundaries, or only loosely relevant just to make the Cafe look busy.

**No strong match yet**

Stay open until 3:30 PM, adjust today’s intent, or try again another visit.

---

## Trust before growth

# No surprise pitches.

Sales, recruiting, fundraising, investor-founder, customer, and partnership conversations stay off unless both people permit the category and there is a credible reason to meet.

---

## Low pressure by design

# You stay in control.

A session is temporary. You can change your mind without creating a public rejection or opening an inbox.

You can skip, ask to be reminded shortly, pause, block, or report.

If either person skips, the introduction becomes inactive for both. Corgi does not say who skipped or why.

---

## Centralized Corgi profile

# One profile, private by default.

Confirm your professional context once, then choose what you want from Corgi. Today’s intent, availability, and boundaries still expire with the session.

- No public member directory
- No follower graph or automatic message thread
- No inferred permission from your background
- Private-community participation remains a separate choice

---

## What Corgi learns

# Built for useful conversations.

The product learns from what happened—not from connection counts. Feedback is private and collected from each person independently.

After an introduction, Corgi may ask:

- Did you meet?
- Was the conversation useful?
- Did it match the reason?
- Did a boundary get crossed?
